reviewer opened every cited `file:symbol` and found no stale or wrong citation, and the
quoted `llk-001` passages reproduce verbatim. What blocked it was the layer the spec's own
`reference:` field flags as unvalidated.

1. **§2.1 and §2.4 stated GTFS wrongly, and Phase 1's parser is built from that table.**
   `stop_lat`/`stop_lon` are Conditionally Required, not required; `location_type` has
   five values, not two, and 2/3/4 are exactly the rows that may carry no coordinates. A
   parser typed off the reflex reading hard-fails on any feed with entrances. Closed with
   a nullability column, an `Option` rule, and a read-only-0-and-1 rule.
2. **Which `stops.txt` rows become stations was never stated, and §2.1 and Phase 1 gave
   different answers.** One reading emits parent rows as stray isolated markers, the other
   does not; they give different station counts and a different picture. Closed with one
   rule — a station is emitted iff a kept line references it, identity current — which
   holds unchanged across both phases.
3. **Phase 1's `stop_sequence` assertion was vacuous.** Non-consecutive values in
   ascending row order are read identically by a correct reader and by one that ignores
   `stop_sequence` entirely — the one behaviour §2.1 goes out of its way to mandate. Only
   rows written *out* of sequence order discriminate. The sharpest catch of the round.
4. **"A missing or unusable colour" was undefined and §2.4 contradicted §1.1.** GTFS
   defaults an omitted `route_color` to `FFFFFF`, so "missing" and "white" are one value
   downstream and two cells in the file. Closed with an exact predicate and a fully
   specified palette, and in §1.1's direction: an explicitly white line stays white,
   because §1.1 assigns that editorial call to the person.
